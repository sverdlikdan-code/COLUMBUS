# Jujutsu Shenanigans Player Community Research — August 2026

> **Research Focus:** What do JJS players care about most? Player interests, pain points, content preferences, and tool demand.

## Executive Summary

Jujutsu Shenanigans (JJS) is a Roblox fighting game with 191k+ active Discord members. The community is highly engaged with **tier lists, custom movesets, balance patches, and skill-building content**. Players prioritize meta builds, competitive PvP, learning curves, and custom moveset sharing. The external moveset builder tool (JJS Skill Builder at `jjsbuilder.com`) is well-established with 300+ community builds published.

---

## TOP 5 PLAYER INTERESTS — Ranked by Discussion Volume

### 1. **Meta Builds & Character Tier Lists** (Highest engagement)
- **Dominance:** Tier lists consistently rank as #1 most-viewed YouTube content in the JJS space
- **Top Tier Characters (July 2026 meta):**
  - **S+ Tier:** Ten Shadows (Megumi), Blood Manipulator (Choso), Cursed Partners (Yuta), Perfection (Mahito), Puppet Master (Mechamaru)
  - **S Tier:** Restless Gambler (Hakari), Star Rage (Yuki), Honored One (Gojo)
  - **Emerging:** Disaster Plants (Hanami) — recently climbed after gaining real domain
- **Pattern:** Tier lists change every patch; meta shifts are driven by character nerfs/buffs, not game-wide changes
- **Player Behavior:** Communities actively debate "is this character skilled or no-skill?" and argue best-in-slot builds

### 2. **Custom Moveset Building & Sharing**
- **Tool Adoption:** JJS Skill Builder (jjsbuilder.com) is the de-facto standard
  - 300+ community-published builds on `jjsbuilder.com/builds/`
  - Moveset codes are **base64-encoded** strings users copy/paste into the in-game Skill Builder
  - Requires "Custom Character" gamepass to import
- **Sharing Channels:** Discord servers, Workshop publishing, Private Server invites
- **Player Pain Point:** Moveset code discovery/curation — players search for "best builds" and want curated recommendations
- **High Interest Indicator:** YouTube videos titled "I Found the BEST Skill Builder in JJS" rank well

### 3. **Game Balance & Patch Reactions**
- **Recent Controversy:** Naoya nerfs (v1.62) sparked visible community backlash ("RIP Naoya mains")
- **Patch Format:** "The Nothing Update" series (v1.45, v1.62, etc.) — developer pattern of hiding some buffs/nerfs for community discovery
- **Most-Changed Characters:** Star Rage, Honored One, Cursed Partners, Defense Attorney, Aspiring Mangaka, Vessel
- **Player Complaint Theme:** Inconsistent balance — some characters feel oppressive (e.g., Ten Shadows considered "near-broken all-rounder")

### 4. **Learning Combat Fundamentals (Skill Gap)**
- **Biggest Barrier:** Massive skill gap between beginners and experienced players
- **Most Important Technique:** Ragdoll Cancel (escape stun by pressing Q+M1 mid-knockdown)
- **Complexity:** Game is deeper than most Roblox anime games; button-mashing loses every time
- **Common Beginner Frustration:** Energy management, understanding neutral game, timing abilities
- **Recommended Entry Characters:** Yuji (Vessel) or Gojo (Honored One) due to straightforward kits
- **Content Demand:** Beginner guides have dedicated guide pages on wiki + multiple YouTube tutorials

### 5. **Competitive PvP Rankings & Tournaments**
- **Meta Focus:** Combos, neutral game, survivability, awakening strength, consistency
- **Domain Expansions:** Highest-impact moves; 120-second cooldown; drive competitive play
- **Competitive Advantage:** Characters with strong neutral pressure + reliable combo extensions dominate
- **Tournament Activity:** No major formal tournaments found in search results, but 1v1 duels and server rankings are primary competitive format

## IMPLICATIONS FOR JJS MOVESET GENERATOR TOOL

### Demand Signals — GREEN LIGHTS
✅ **High:** External build tool adoption (jjsbuilder.com succeeds with 300+ builds)

### Use Cases for Generator Tool

2. **Build counter-picking** — "This character counter-picks Gojo builds"

  - AI-powered recommendations (vs. manual browsing of 300 builds)
  - Playstyle matching (aggressive/defensive)
  - Matchup analysis (counters X character)
  - Beginner safety (pre-validated builds)

  2. **Beginner learners** — Want guided, explained builds
