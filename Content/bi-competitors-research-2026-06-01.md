---

## Language and Tone They Use

- **Hebrew, technical, feature-listing.** Most sites lead with tool names (Power BI, Qlik, Alteryx) rather than outcomes.
- **Volume signals as proof:** "200 clients", "300 projects", "17 years" — quantity over quality of work.
- **No emotional hook.** Zero differentiation language around the *why* — all describe *what* they implement.
- **BINA-BI** comes closest to strategic language: "accountants and analysts who tailor BI to executives" — but the site execution is generic.
- **No one uses the word "craft."** No one talks about data culture, decision-making speed, or executive trust.

---

## Presentation Style (What's Visible)

- Mostly service-listing pages. No portfolio showcases, no screenshot galleries of their dashboards.
- No pitch decks publicly visible.
- Color palette: corporate blues and grays, stock-photo heavy.
- Global premium reference: McKinsey QuantumBlack / Accenture-style firms lead with **business outcome language** ("2x faster decisions", "30% cost reduction") + case study PDFs with named clients. Israeli players do not do this.

---

## What NO ONE in Israel Is Saying

1. **"We build reporting cultures, not just reports"** — nobody talks about adoption, change management, or whether dashboards are actually used after delivery.
2. **"Microsoft Fabric is a platform transformation, not a tool upgrade"** — zero Israeli firms are positioned on the modern Fabric stack as a strategic shift.
3. **Measurable ROI language** — no one publishes outcomes: revenue impact, time saved, decision cycles shortened.
4. **Premium / boutique identity** — the market is entirely mid-market generalists. No "fewer clients, deeper work" positioning exists.

---

## 3 Positioning Directions That Would Stand Out

**Direction 1 — The Outcome Firm**
> "We don't deliver dashboards. We deliver decisions."
Lead with case studies that show business impact (revenue, cost, cycle time). Refuse to pitch features. Position every deliverable against a business KPI.

**Direction 2 — The Fabric Transformation Partner**
> "Microsoft Fabric is not a BI upgrade — it's a data platform reset. We move you there."
Own the Microsoft Fabric narrative in Israel. Be the firm executives call when they want to understand what Fabric means for their org — before procurement happens.

**Direction 3 — The Executive Intelligence Studio**
> "BI for people who make decisions, not people who run reports."
Boutique positioning: fewer clients, higher-touch, focus on C-suite and board-level reporting. Premium pricing, named-partner model, visible methodology. Design-led output (award-worthy visuals).

---

## Key Takeaway

The Israeli BI consulting market is technically competent and undifferentiated. The entire market speaks the same language: tools, volume, years of experience. The white space is **strategic, outcome-led, design-premium positioning** — which is exactly where BIZNES-AI can win without competing on price.
